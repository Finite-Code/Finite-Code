### 👨‍💻 About Me
I'm a Class 11 student, custom ROM developer, and tech content creator passionate about low-level systems and hardware tinkering. Whether I'm building AOSP-based operating systems, flashing firmware onto ESP32 microcontrollers, or editing tech videos, I love turning ideas into functional projects.
